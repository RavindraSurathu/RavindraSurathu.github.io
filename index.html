About
I keep systems running, and I automate the work that keeps them that way.

I started in Windows and IIS administration and grew into the engineer teams call when production is on fire: vulnerability remediation, certificate lifecycles, load balancers, migrations and root-cause analysis across Production, UAT, DR and Development.

Over time the repetitive work became something to automate. Today I patch fleets of servers with Ansible and PowerShell, run workloads on AWS, and configure identity with Microsoft Entra ID. Most recently I've supported a baggage reconciliation platform used at international airports, where 99.9% uptime is the baseline.

I'm also extending that work into Infrastructure as Code and containers with Terraform, Docker and Kubernetes, shown in my BagTrace project on GitHub.
